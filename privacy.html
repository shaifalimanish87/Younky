Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DD Month YYYY]

Younky Eyewear ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide at checkout — name, mobile number, and delivery address.
  • Mobile number verification (OTP) used to confirm genuine orders.
  • Order details — items purchased, order value, and payment method selected.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order via WhatsApp, SMS, or phone.
  • To improve our products and website experience.

Payments

Payments are processed via UPI or Razorpay. We do not store your card, bank, or UPI credentials on our servers — these are handled directly by the respective payment provider.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share necessary order details with delivery partners to fulfil your order.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at support@younky.com.

Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, reach out via our Contact page.
